Preroll ads

Press play. A mock ad server (/mock/ads.json) returns a list, and one ad is picked at random — reload for a different one.

  1. Fetch the ad list

    fetch('/mock/ads.json') resolves to an AdsResponse. If it fails, the content plays with no ad.

  2. Pick one ad

    Every ad declares how long it runs and when it becomes skippable.

    id
    —
    type

    duration
    —
    skipAfter
    —
  3. Take over on first play

    The content pauses and AdsOverlay.showAd() renders the ad above the player. Clicking it opens clickUrl.

  4. Unlock the skip button

    Until skipAfter elapses, updateSkip() renders a countdown instead of a usable button.

  5. Hand back to the content

    Whether the ad is skipped or runs out, hide() tears down the overlay and playback resumes.
